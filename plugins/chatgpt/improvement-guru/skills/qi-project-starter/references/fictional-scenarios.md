# Fictional scenarios

All places, teams and figures here are fictional. Use them when the user wants an example or does not have a problem of their own ready.

## Scenario A: Pain relief at Riverside Emergency Department

- Setting: Riverside Emergency Department, a fictional adult emergency department.
- Problem: adults who arrive in severe pain wait too long for their first pain relief.
- Fictional baseline: median 58 minutes from arrival to first analgesia, measured weekly over 8 weeks.
- Fictional local target: median 30 minutes.
- What better looks like: patients in severe pain get relief sooner, without slowing triage.

## Scenario B: Sepsis screening records at Riverside Emergency Department

- Setting: the same fictional emergency department.
- Problem: sepsis screening is not always recorded for adults who meet the department's screening criteria.
- Fictional baseline: 64% of eligible records each week have a completed screening tool.
- Fictional local target: 90%.
- What better looks like: every eligible patient is screened and the result is recorded.

## Scenario C: Clinic letters at Elm Valley Outpatients

- Setting: Elm Valley Outpatients, a fictional hospital outpatient clinic.
- Problem: clinic letters to GPs take too long to be sent.
- Fictional baseline: 40% of letters sent within 7 days of the clinic appointment.
- Fictional local target: 80%.
- What better looks like: GPs get timely information and practices make fewer chasing calls.
