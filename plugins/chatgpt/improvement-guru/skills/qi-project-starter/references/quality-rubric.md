# Quality rubric

Use each section at the stage named in `SKILL.md`, and run the Final checks before every final reply.

## Work type

- **QI:** testing changes to improve a local process or outcome, measured over time. Continue with the workflow.
- **Clinical audit:** measuring current practice against an existing, explicit standard, then re-auditing. Point to HQIP's Best Practice in Clinical Audit in `uk-sources.md`.
- **Service evaluation:** judging how well a current service works, without testing a change.
- **Research:** aims to produce new, generalisable knowledge. In the UK it usually needs research ethics and HRA review; the local research and development (R&D) office can advise.
- **Incident learning:** responding to a specific patient-safety incident. In England this goes through the local PSIRF route and is outside this workflow.

If the type is unclear, say so and suggest the user confirms it with the local QI or audit team.

## SMART checks

- **Specific:** one measure, one population or pathway, one setting.
- **Measurable:** baseline and target are both numbers, with units.
- **Achievable:** the target is plausible for a first project phase. Say so if it looks ambitious and suggest an interim target.
- **Relevant:** linked to safety, effectiveness, patient experience, timeliness, efficiency or equity.
- **Time-bound:** a calendar date, usually three to six months away for a first project.

## Measures checks

- One outcome measure, one or two process measures and one balancing measure.
- Every measure has an operational definition precise enough that two people would count the same way.
- Numerator and denominator, or start and stop times, are stated.
- The collection frequency gives at least 10 data points before the aim date, where possible.
- No data item identifies a person.

## PDSA checks

- One change, at a small scale, for a stated length of time.
- A written prediction.
- Adopt, adapt and abandon thresholds set before the test starts.
- A stop rule for safety or workload.
- The proposed change is to a process. Clinical treatment, prescribing and protocols stay outside this workflow.

## Final checks

- All project figures are made up and labelled fictional. No real service figure or estimate is echoed or used.
- Every cited source is from `uk-sources.md`, with its link.
- Nothing local was invented; unknowns are marked [to confirm locally].
- No identifiable or case-level detail appears anywhere in the pack.
- Local checks are shown as not yet done, never as confirmed.
- The Limits section is present and unchanged.
